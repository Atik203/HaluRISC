# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can report a problem, ask a
question, or propose a change without being harassed. We welcome contributors
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a motive.
- Give reproducible examples instead of general complaints.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's data, email, or credentials.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, in issue trackers, in pull requests, and in
any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the corresponding author at
**mrahaman2310298@bscse.uiu.ac.bd**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers
may warn, remove content, or block an account, depending on severity.
