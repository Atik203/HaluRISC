Dear Sir / Madam,

\begin{enumerate}
  \item I am happy to submit an \textbf{original article} titled ``\textbf{Calibrated Hallucination Risk for Large Language Models Under Domain and Task Shift with Evidence-Consistent XGBoost}'' for consideration of publication in the \emph{[Journal Name]}.

  \item This manuscript introduces \textbf{EC-XGB}, an evidence-consistent XGBoost model that scores hallucination risk from 35 features: 26 base features, eight claim-level natural language inference aggregates, and a source indicator. The model is trained on HaluEval QA and RAGTruth non-QA rows, and evaluated with fixed-hyperparameter ablations, source and target calibration, conformal prediction, SHAP stability analysis, a length-only control, and judge stress tests. It reaches F1 0.9854 and AUROC 0.9986 on HaluEval QA, but lexical overlap and length carry most of that score. On MedHallu it ranks the hallucinated answer above the grounded one in 86.7\% of 1{,}000 pairs while flagging every row (ECE 0.493). Target Platt calibration lowers ECE from 0.731 to 0.125 on RAGTruth QA without changing the ranking.

  \item We believe this manuscript is appropriate for publication in the \emph{[Journal Name]} because it separates ranking from probability under domain and task shift, reports the negative results as well, and provides a reproducible protocol with public code and data instructions.
