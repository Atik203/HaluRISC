HaluRISC demo evidence file
================================

Upload this file on the chat page (Evidence button) or paste a passage into
Analyze mode, then run the demo cases. Each paragraph below is one passage. The
claim checker retrieves from these passages, so the grounded cases should come
back supported and the hallucinated cases should come back contradicted.

oceans. It has been established that the region is at its warmest for at least
40,000 years, and the Arctic-wide melt season has lengthened at a rate of 5 days
per decade from 1979 to 2013, dominated by a later autumn freezeup. The melt
season grows longer because the autumn freezeup now starts later in the year.

physician and microbiologist, and the discovery of penicillin opened the era of
antibiotics.

during the period of the Mexican Civil War. Agnes Salm-Salm was the American
wife of Prince Felix zu Salm-Salm.
